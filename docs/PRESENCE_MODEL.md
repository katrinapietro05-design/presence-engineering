# Presence Model (Research Scaffold)

## Model boundary

The source manuscript frames Presence as an architectural variable and proposes representing it through state, temporal persistence, relational participation, observability, and continuity. This repository organizes that framing for later validation; it does not claim universal measurement or empirical confirmation.

## Registers to keep distinct

- state at a time point;
- change or transition across time;
- relation to an environment, signal, or other system;
- observation and measurement process;
- continuity and memory representation;
- provenance and validation status.

## Research controls

Every quantity must define units, operationalization, population or system boundary, source, uncertainty, and validation method. Human participants require applicable consent, privacy protections, and review. Synthetic examples must be labeled synthetic.
